Seeraga samba biryani at Hotel Mudaliyar

சீரக சம்பா பிரியாணி

Tamil Nadu biryani is made with small seeraga samba rice. Here is the full biryani section of the menu.

Hotel Mudaliyar · 8 October 2026

Seeraga samba is a short-grain, fragrant rice from Tamil Nadu. The grains are small, about the size of cumin seeds (seeragam), which is where the name comes from. It soaks up masala in a way long-grain basmati doesn't, and it is the rice of Tamil biryani.

What to order with it

From the starters: Mutton Chukka, Chettinadu Chicken, Kaadai Roast or Fish Fry. For one person, a biryani and one starter is a full meal.

Biryani is served at lunch (12:00–3:30 pm) and dinner (6:00–11:00 pm). For functions, the kitchen also caters biryani in bulk at Ammaiyappan Hall.
