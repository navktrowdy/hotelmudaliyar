Madurai Spl. curry dosa: a dosa with the curry cooked on top

மதுரை கறி தோசை

Madurai is known for meat curry cooked straight onto the dosa. Here are the five versions served at Hotel Mudaliyar.

Hotel Mudaliyar · 8 October 2026

Order a kari dosai in Madurai and the curry doesn't come in a bowl on the side. It is cooked onto the dosa itself. At Hotel Mudaliyar this section of the menu is called Madurai Spl. Curry Dosa.

The five curry dosas

Chicken Curry Dosa₹300
Boti Curry Dosa₹320
Prawn Curry Dosa₹330
Mutton Curry Dosa₹350
Liver Curry Dosa₹350

Boti (goat intestine) and liver are Madurai favourites, and many restaurants don't serve them. If it's your first time, start with chicken or mutton.

When to order

Curry dosas are served at breakfast (7:00–11:30 am) and dinner (6:00–11:00 pm). One is a full meal for one person. Two people can share one along with a plain or podi dosa.

Other dosas on the menu

The Dosa Corner has 16 more dosas, from a plain dosa at ₹80 to ghee roast, rava masala, egg dosa and egg adai dosa.
