Planning a function at Ammaiyappan Hall

அம்மையப்பன் ஹால்

An air-conditioned hall above the restaurant, with food from the Mudaliyar kitchen downstairs.

Hotel Mudaliyar · 8 October 2026

Ammaiyappan Hall is the air-conditioned function hall upstairs at Hotel Mudaliyar on Pandi Kovil Ring Road, Melamadai. The restaurant kitchen is in the same building, so your guests get the same food the restaurant serves.

Occasions

  • Wedding receptions and betrothal (nichayathartham) lunches
  • Birthdays, naming ceremonies and family get-togethers
  • Company lunches, team dinners and small meetings
  • Get-togethers for college and school alumni

Menu ideas

Build your menu from the restaurant's own dishes:

  • Morning functions: idly, mini sambar idly, idiyappam, dosa and muttai idli
  • Lunch: veg meals, or seeraga samba chicken or mutton biryani with Chettinadu chicken or mutton chukka
  • Dinner: parotta with salna, kothu parotta, and curry dosa

If you want a dish that isn't on the restaurant menu, ask the kitchen when you book.

How to book

Call 0452 253 0303 or ask at the counter. Have your date, lunch or dinner slot, an approximate guest count, and veg/non-veg preference ready. More details are on the hall page.
