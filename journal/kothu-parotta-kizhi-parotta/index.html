Kothu, kizhi and veechu: the parottas of Madurai

கொத்து பரோட்டா · கிழி பரோட்டா

Madurai is a parotta city. Here is how the parottas on the Hotel Mudaliyar menu differ.

Hotel Mudaliyar · 8 October 2026

Kothu parotta

For kothu parotta, parotta is chopped on the tawa with two steel blades. You hear the clatter before the plate reaches you. It is tossed with onion, masala and salna, plus egg or meat.

Veg Kothu Parotta₹130
Egg Kothu Parotta₹150
Chicken Kothu Parotta₹300
Mutton Kothu Parotta₹350

Kizhi parotta

Kizhi means a wrapped bundle. The parotta is wrapped with curry so it soaks up the gravy.

Chicken Kizhi Parotta₹250
Mutton Kizhi Parotta₹300

Madurai Spl., veechu and poricha

The breads are priced per two pieces. Plain Parotta is ₹60. Madurai Spl., Veechu (thin and stretched) and Poricha (fried) parottas are ₹80 each. Egg Veechu is ₹100 and Chilli Parotta is ₹130.

Labba

Egg Labba (₹130), Chicken Labba (₹250) and Mutton Labba (₹300) are on the menu too. The same chopped style is also made with idiyappam: see Idiyappam Varieties.

Parottas are served in the evening and at dinner, from 6:00 to 11:00 pm.
